# Treksor profile and evidence review

The first release uses declared preferences to choose which tool to surface. It asks for a focus, then occasionally asks about available training days, cooking time or whether the focus has changed. Answers are editable, scoped to the signed-in account and stored on the device. A user can pause the questions. Home presents at most one question every 28 days, including after dismissal.

A muscle goal opens the existing training questionnaire. The user chooses whether to prefill the relevant answers and confirms any saved plan change. A weight goal surfaces meal organization tools. Meal ideas are available to every user. A preparation-time preference can narrow the first view and the full catalogue remains available.

## Three review gates for future plan rules

1. **Evidence review.** Record the specific recommendation, source version, outcome, strength of evidence and population studied. Check applicability to Treksor's audience and document uncertainty. ACSM's 2026 position stand synthesizes resistance training evidence for healthy adults. CDC describes realistic weight goals and reviewing habits over time. [ACSM position stand](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41843416/) · [CDC weight management guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/healthy-weight-growth/losing-weight/index.html)
2. **Independent professional review.** A qualified reviewer signs off on the intended population, contraindications, wording and when users should be directed to professional support. NIDDK's Body Weight Planner illustrates how a weight tool can limit its intended audience and distinguish guidance from medical care. [NIDDK Body Weight Planner](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/bwp)
3. **Product and software review.** Test edge cases, missing and conflicting records, permission changes, account switching, explanations and reversibility. Require a reviewable proposal and explicit acceptance before replacing an active plan. Record the date, rule version and reviewer decision before activation.

Personal calorie targets, clinical nutrition and automatic changes to workload require the above gates. The current profile routes users to existing tools and exposes their own preferences for review. The references establish the rationale for this limited scope. Professional review of future prescriptive rules is pending.
